# Design guidelines

Use blush pink (#F5D5E0), lavender (#E5DDF8), sky blue (#DDEDF9), cloud white (#FFF9F5) and muted plum (#59465F). Keep page colors in site.css theme tokens. Muted plum carries dark text and action emphasis. Night surfaces use deep plum with pale text.

Cormorant Garamond sets editorial headings and italic emphasis. Inter sets labels, controls and body copy. The desktop build bundles both fonts. Georgia and system sans-serif serve as local fallbacks.

The circular AP seal carries a ribbon and dotted inner ring. Use the pale-ink version against plum. Repeat the emblem in the header, abbreviated entrance and footer. The cloud atlas uses an arched figure plate, a consistent isometric projection and separate palettes for each project. Keep the résumé single-column and professional.

Provide descriptive links, visible focus rings, touch-friendly controls, a skip link and text alternatives to illustrated objects. The floating dock preserves bottom space. Theme preferences are auto/day/night; automatic mode switches at 06:00 and 18:00 visitor time. Reduce-motion bypasses entrance, floating motion, parallax, custom cursor and 3D loading. The cloud cursor supplements the operating-system pointer. Keep the desk on demand with a pixel ratio capped at 1.5.
